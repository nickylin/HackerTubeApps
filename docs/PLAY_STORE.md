**Full description:**

Tired of YouTube recommendations? HackerTube is built the opposite way.

In October 2026 Andrej Karpathy mined 20 years of Hacker News comments and kept
only the videos people there genuinely recommended: 6,454 videos across 150
topics — talks, courses, demos and documentaries that survived two decades of
the toughest audience on the internet.

This open-source Android client brings that library to your phone, tablet and
Android TV:

• Home feed and curated playlists like "The HN canon"
• Every video shows WHY Hacker News recommends it, with real quotes
• Search and browse 150 topics
• Liked, Watch Later and History — stored only on your device
• No account. No ads. No tracking. Videos play right in the app.

Open source (MIT): https://github.com/nickylin/HackerTubeApps
